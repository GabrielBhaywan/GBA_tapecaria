## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, high-conversion visual language tailored for bespoke upholstery, maritime outfitting, luxury automotive interiors, and corporate entrance design. The aesthetic combines the disciplined clarity of modern editorial layouts with the tactile richness of nautical lifestyle design.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Bespoke Precision:** Evokes rigorous craftsmanship, marine-grade durability, and tailored millimeter perfection.
- **Executive Luxury:** Channels the serene, expansive feeling of high-end yachting and prestige automotive finishes through deep oceanic depths and crisp coastal contrast.
- **Immediate Action & Confidence:** Gives commercial buyers, boat owners, and automotive enthusiasts direct, frictionless pathways to request custom quotes and start consultations via WhatsApp.

### Design Movement
The style blends **Corporate Modern minimalism** with **Refined Nautical Tactility**:
- Crisp, asymmetric structural layouts anchored by generous negative space.
- Alternating dark oceanic chambers and pure alabaster expanses, reminiscent of open water flowing into sleek yacht decks.
- Floating stat cards, rounded pill accents, and precise metallic highlight rings echoing bespoke marine hardware.

## Colors

The palette directly honors maritime architecture and technical upholstery materials, contrasting midnight navy and oceanic blues against pristine white and energetic conversion accents.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#07172B` - Abyss Navy):** Deepest oceanic anchor. Used for primary typography, authoritative headers, dark architectural sections, and premium container backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#0D549E` - Cobalt Wave):** The signature vibrant blue from the brand identity. Used for category navigation badges, key feature highlights, secondary button fills, and directional indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366` - WhatsApp Vibrant Emerald):** Reserved strictly for high-conversion actions—WhatsApp consultation buttons, live estimate CTAs, and active direct messaging prompts.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F4F7FA` - Deck Alabaster):** An ultra-clean, slightly cool off-white that prevents harsh glare while delivering maximum contrast against `#07172B`.
- **Pure Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** High-light surface for cards, imagery frames, and contrast modules.
- **Muted Steel (`#64748B` / `#94A3B8`):** Subtle utility grays for metadata, specification tables, and supportive captions.

## Typography

The type system balances technical engineering with contemporary luxury:

- **Headline Family (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a geometric, precise, and subtly industrial aesthetic that reflects custom laser cuts, structured embroidery, and naval engineering. Used in uppercase or tight title case for dominant section headers.
- **Body & Label Family (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, crisp, and exceptionally legible across digital screen resolutions. Renders commercial specifications, long-form craft explanations, and conversion button labels with effortless clarity.
- **Uppercase Badges:** Category overlines (`label-sm`, `label-md`) must always carry positive letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic embossed luxury marine branding plates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display large, high-resolution product photography alongside concise quote forms and benefit matrices.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin` = `1.5rem`, `gutter` = `1rem`. Stacked vertical composition for full thumb accessibility to primary contact triggers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin-md` = `3rem`, `gutter` = `1.5rem`. Two-column hero reflow and paired category cards.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, max-width `1280px` centered, `margin-lg` = `6rem`, `gutter-lg` = `2rem`. Allows generous side margins, overlapping floating metrics, and asymmetrical photo collages.

### Rhythm & Vertical Flow
- Transitions between alternating dark-blue and off-white background sections utilize smooth 48px to 80px curved wave or diagonal mask dividers to recall yacht wakes and water movement.
- Component groupings respect an 8pt baseline cadence (`space-sm` for tight icon/label lockups, `space-md` for text blocks, `space-xl` for card interiors).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean atmospheric depth rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows, keeping product photography central and crisp:

- **Level 1 (Surface Cards on White Canvas):** Minimal lift with an ultra-diffused tint: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(7, 23, 43, 0.06)`. Bordered by a delicate 1px border `rgba(7, 23, 43, 0.08)` to ground the container.
- **Level 2 (Floating Floating Metric Badges & Floating Nav):** Glassmorphic backdrops for overlays positioned above product photography (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6); box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(7, 23, 43, 0.12)`).
- **Level 3 (Dark Field Elevation):** In primary deep navy sections, elevation is expressed via tonal luminescence: surface containers sit on `#0E233D` accompanied by subtle top-edge hairpins (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- **CTA Luminance:** The WhatsApp primary conversion trigger features a subtle directional emerald glow on hover (`0 8px 24px -2px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern radii (`roundedness: 2` — `8px` default corner radius, `16px` for large cards, and full pill capsules for badges and buttons).

- **Buttons & Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to match high-end nautical aesthetics, streamline thumb-tap hit areas, and mirror circular maritime emblems.
- **Showcase & Image Cards:** `rounded-lg` (`16px`) corners with hidden overflow, providing polished framing for close-up embroidery, marine vinyl, and custom mat weaves.
- **Stat Badges & Micro-tags:** Pill capsules with refined internal padding (`6px` vertical, `14px` horizontal).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary WhatsApp Conversion Button:** Pill shape, vibrant emerald background (`#25D366`), white bold text, integrated WhatsApp vector mark on the left. Hover activates subtle scale (`scale-102`) and soft emerald glow.
- **Secondary Action Button:** Navy pill (`#07172B`) with crisp white label and subtle right arrow (`→`) in light-mode areas; inverted white pill with navy text when placed within dark maritime blocks.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with 1px border (`#0D549E` or `rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`), designed for portfolio filtering and secondary documentation downloads.

### Service & Product Cards
- **Dark Chamber Cards:** Built on deep navy surfaces with subtle 1px stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), featuring large centered line-art or metallic icons (Anchor, Steering Wheel, Embroidered Mat, Fleet) followed by short high-impact copy.
- **Portfolio Showcase Cards:** Full-bleed photograph of custom work (yacht deck, luxury car interior, commercial entrance) overlaying an inner bottom gradient vignette, housing title, subtitle, and a circular directional tap trigger.

### Metric Callout Badges
- Floating compact white boxes placed overlapping the corner of primary imagery (e.g., "100+ Projetos Náuticos Entregues", "10+ Anos de Garantia"). Features bold Space Grotesk numerals paired with concise two-line labels.

### Input Fields & Instant Quote Modules
- Clean, tall inputs (`48px` minimum height) with light gray-blue backgrounds (`#F4F7FA`), subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), and focused states utilizing the `#0D549E` cobalt glow. WhatsApp phone input pre-formatted with Brazilian country code format (+55).

### Sticky Conversion Floating Bar (Mobile)
- Fixed bottom bar on viewports `< 768px` featuring quick-action click-to-WhatsApp and telephone callout to maximize instant lead capture while reviewing catalog items.
